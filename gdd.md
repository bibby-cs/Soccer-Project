Description

Description: A simple 2D soccer game where the player controls one soccer player and tries to score more goals than the opposing team. The player can move around the field, pass, shoot, and steal the ball. Each match has a short time limit, keeping the gameplay simple and fast


Core Gameplay Loop

The Gameplay Loop:
Start the match.
Move the player toward the ball.
Pass or dribble toward the opponent’s goal.
Shoot the ball.
Try to prevent the opponent from scoring.
Score goals and continue until time runs out.
The team with the most goals wins.

MDA Framework

Mechanics:
The player controls a soccer player using keyboard controls. The player can move, sprint, pass, shoot, and tackle. The opposing team uses simple AI to chase the ball and attempt to score.

Dynamics:
Players compete for possession and try to create scoring opportunities. The player must decide whether to pass, dribble, or shoot depending on their position on the field.

Player Experience

How should they feel? (Incorporate Leblanc’s Taxonomy of pleasures):
The game should feel simple, competitive, and satisfying. Players should feel rewarded when they successfully pass the ball, score a goal, or stop the opponent from scoring.
The game mainly uses:
Challenge: Players try to score more goals than the opponent.
Sensation: Players experience movement, kicking, goals, and sound effects.
Fellowship: Players can compete against another player if multiplayer is implemented.
Expression: Players can choose whether to pass, dribble, or shoot.

Target Audience (Incorporate Bartle’s Taxonomy):
The game is designed for casual players and students who want a simple soccer game that is easy to learn.
Killers: Players who enjoy competing against an opponent.
Achievers: Players who enjoy scoring goals and winning matches.
Socializers: Players who enjoy playing against friends.
Explorers: Players who enjoy experimenting with different ways to score.
Progression Over Time
The game does not require a complicated progression system. Players can improve by learning the controls and becoming better at passing, shooting, defending, and positioning.
Optional progression could include:
Increasing opponent difficulty.
Unlocking different soccer teams.
Longer or more difficult matches.
