# Attribution

## AI Use

ChatGPT was used to assist with the development of the Game Design Document (GDD).

AI was used for:
- Brainstorming ideas for a simple soccer game.
- Helping organize the GDD according to the provided template.
- Assisting with descriptions of the gameplay loop, mechanics, MDA framework, player experience, and target audience.
- Editing and improving the wording of the GDD.

The final game design decisions and implementation were made by the student.
